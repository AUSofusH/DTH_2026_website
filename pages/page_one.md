## The Advantages of open source software: 

### Response time: 

Depending on the size of the project and number of contributors, open source projects can rapidly identify bugs and roll out bugfixes. Users of the software can very easily report issues, in the case of Github, through the "issue tracker", and other users experiencing similar problems are able to see if the issue is known, and whether a fix is in progress (Github, 2025). If a fix is not actively worked on, a skilled user may even submit their own fix to be merged into the project, via Github's "pull request" feature, which lets the repository members review and add outside contributions quickly (Github, 2026).  

### Competition: 

This flexibility combined with the often passionate work ethic of many open-source developers increasingly challenges commercial software providers to either improve their product, or risk paying customers choosing open-source alternatives. Recently, Adobe's increasingly predatory business practices and advances in AI-driven software decompiling, have lead to a disgruntled team of Adobe Creative Cloud users reverse engineering and subsequently open-sourcing many of Adobe's most famous products, like Photoshop and Premiere Pro. Even though the Adobe Creative Cloud is closed source, the developers of Artcraft managed to reverse engineer and effectively clone Adobe's software through the use of Claude AI and vibe-coding (Wilkins, 2026). While the legality of this practice is questionable, it nonetheless forces Adobe to act, or risk hemorrhaging paying customers, who have waited years for software alternatives with similar features and refinement. If a superior commercial software was to appear on the market, larger competing companies usually buy them out and retire the software to maintain a market monopoly. This is less of a risk with open source software.
